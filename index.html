RULE SIGNALS
    01

    How anomaly scoring works

    Session features are compared with 240 deterministically generated normal sessions. A 5-nearest-neighbour distance is measured against a synthetic baseline distribution.

    02

    Transparent policy decisions

    The anomaly signal is combined with interpretable policy flags, producing a trust score and one of three actions: allow, require step-up MFA, or block.

    03

    Research limitations

    This is an educational proof-of-concept, not a validated intrusion detector. All behavior is synthetic; scores are not probabilities, and no live access is enforced.
